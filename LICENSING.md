## AI-generated assets

- **Higgsfield (Tripo text-to-3D models, and Meshy animation-library clips through Higgsfield's rigging tool):** generated on Kiran Mohan's paid Higgsfield Starter subscription in September 2026 (account evidence: the plan and its subscription-credit grants as the Higgsfield integration reported them).
  - Higgsfield's Terms of Use (§4.4, as summarised in its help centre, "Who owns my generations and can I use them commercially?") say Higgsfield claims no ownership of outputs and doesn't restrict their commercial use, independent of plan.
  - Tripo and Meshy both give paid users commercial rights to their outputs; Meshy's terms also cover animation-library assets built into a project.
  - Neither upstream engine documents how its terms apply when used through Higgsfield. These assets are redistributed here on that basis, with no further warranty.
  - **Higgsfield's terms forbid using its outputs, or data derived from them, to train, fine-tune or distil AI models.** So these files, and the images that show them, are **not** offered under CC BY 4.0. Kiran grants permission to use, modify and redistribute them as part of this game or forks of it, on the condition that they are not used to train, fine-tune or distil AI models.
- **OpenAI image generation** (texture sources): OpenAI's terms assign the output to the user. They are offered under CC BY 4.0 with the original game content.
- Purely machine-generated output may not be protected by copyright in some jurisdictions. Where no rights exist, none are claimed; the condition above is the permission given, not a claim of copyright.
