# Warsaw Downtown: friends playtest

Thanks for playing. Each round takes about 20 minutes. Note anything that feels off. A screenshot plus one line ("what I did → what happened") is perfect.

## Before you start

- **Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop.** Firefox and Safari work but are less tested.
- **Laptops with two graphics chips** (most gaming laptops): Windows Settings › System › Display › Graphics › your browser → **High performance**. On the built-in Intel chip the game drops resolution to keep 60 fps.
- Open the link Kiran sends. Wait for the loading screen, then click **Play**. Esc opens the menu.

## Controls (change any of them in Settings › Controls)

| Key        | Action                            | Key                 | Action                   |
| ---------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------ |
| WASD       | move                              | Shift (double-tap)  | sprint (tactical sprint) |
| Space      | jump / vault                      | C (while sprinting) | crouch (slide)           |
| Z          | prone                             | Mouse 1 / 2         | fire / aim               |
| R          | reload                            | 1 · 2 · 3           | rifle · pistol · knife   |
| Q          | quick knife                       | F                   | block (knife parry)      |
| G          | frag grenade                      | H · J               | smoke · jammer           |
| **B**      | **recon drone** (launch / return) | T                   | radar ping               |
| E          | get in / out of a truck           | N                   | switch truck seat        |
| Tab (hold) | scoreboard                        | X (hold) / Enter    | quick chat / type chat   |

## Things to try, in order

1. **Quick match with bots** (the plain link). Can you move, shoot, reload, switch weapons and knife someone? Does anything stutter or snap?
2. **Recon drone.** Walk over a **▲ drone uplink** (shown on the radar), press **B**, fly with WASD and the mouse, Space up, C down, then B again to come back. Players the drone sees show on your radar.
3. **Trucks.** Find one of the two armoured 4x4s, press E. Drive, swap seats (N), shoot from the back, run someone over.
4. **Friend room.** Kiran presses **Create friend room** and shares the `?room=` link. Everyone joins, Kiran sets bots, weather and time, then **Start match**. Play one full match to the results screen.
5. **Knife-only match** (host setting), then one in **Rain** or **Neon night** weather.
6. **Practice vs bots** (main menu): solo, offline, pauses with the menu.

## Please report

- Anything that broke: you got stuck, fell through the map, lost connection, saw a black screen or an error.
- Moments that felt unfair: shot behind a wall, a hit that didn't count, spawning into a fight.
- Your fps (F3 shows it), your laptop model, and the browser you used.
- The best and worst moment of the session, in a word or two each.
