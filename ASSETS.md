Since the scan is watertight around its torn lips, sharp concave returns and doubled-back sheet edges identify opening rims as well as open boundaries; UV seams never create wear. Distances are evaluated per texel, preventing whole coarse triangles from becoming rust. Fresh baked AO/cavity, height above ground and wheel-arch distance locate corrosion; actual edge/tear donors feed gravity-directed runs. Local cabin/engine heat fields and opening rims locate charcoal soot and subtle tempered steel. Fine world-space noise only breaks up these masks. Rust/soot are rough dielectrics, faded paint stays moderately rough, and sparse exposed steel chips are smoother and partly metallic; millimetre-scale pitting/flaking is baked into the tangent normal. ## Isolated column stone upgrade (2026-10-04)

`tools/assets/source/column-stone-v2.png` is an AI-authored base-colour image generated
with the built-in OpenAI image tool under the user's applicable OpenAI account terms.
It is not a CC0 scan or a measured photogrammetry material. The prompt requested a
flat orthographic seamless pale ivory sandstone surface with fine mineral grain,
small pores, restrained weathering, no stone joints, directional lighting, text or
painted column geometry. Native result: 1254 × 1254 pixels.

`tools/assets/column_stone_maps.py` installs a 1024-square colour texture and infers
millimetre-scale normal, pore occlusion and roughness data for
`packages/client/public/tex/palace-columns-v2/`. It does not upscale the source or
bake an illuminated column image. The manifest records source/output SHA256,
channel convention, bytes and dimensions. The physical relief is inferred rather
than measured. Column sculpture is original procedural project geometry.

On 2026-10-12 the column maps were converted to WebP through the shared encoder,
retaining 1024 × 1024 outputs. Colour uses `match_jpeg=85` after a 0.5-pixel Gaussian
prefilter; inferred normal/ORM data uses a 3-pixel median and 2-pixel Gaussian
prefilter before q92 encoding. The maps initially totaled 252,930 bytes. This reduces fine
material noise; decoded normal angle, roughness error and albedo PSNR are guarded
by `test_column_stone_maps.py`. The packed blue channel is unused: it is zero before
lossy encoding, and the material keeps metalness zero without a metalness map.
These are inferred surface channels; appearance after filtering requires browser review.

Round 4 normalises the filtered albedo's mean in linear light to the facade palette
`#e6d8bf` before encoding. The manifest records the decoded WebP mean and palette;
the asset test requires CIE Lab D65 Delta E 1976 below 1. The maps now total 251,106
bytes. Normal/ORM bytes and their source-derived relief, roughness and cavity remain
unchanged; the shader no longer adds a separate eyeballed hue multiplier.
